# Welcome collage photograph sources

The welcome hero uses 20 distinct Philippine agriculture photographs in five four-photo collages. Eight subjects also appear in existing login/service assets; twelve subjects are new. No photograph repeats within the five collages. These are illustrative scenes, not identified AgriGOV users, assistance recipients, or endorsements.

Source metadata and licenses were checked through Wikimedia Commons on September 20, 2026. Every downloaded JPEG was opened for visual inspection. Files are Commons-provided resized derivatives, with no local content editing or recompression. CSS crops the display; each photograph and any adaptation retain its linked license. Public attribution is in `public/photo-credits.html`, linked from the welcome footer.

## Layout and performance

- Rice country: a large planting image, tall harvest print, and two lower landscape prints.
- People who grow: one portrait composition alongside three smaller landscape prints.
- Life by the water: three smaller photos above a wide fishing-boat image.
- More from our land: three small crop photos beside a tall banana-farm image.
- Around the farm: four staggered prints mixing equipment, corn and livestock.

The 20 JPEGs total 1,947,849 bytes (1.86 MiB). Five main images are 960 pixels wide and fifteen secondary images are 330 pixels wide. All figures reserve space. Only the first collage has image URLs in the initial HTML; JavaScript hydrates the current collage and the upcoming collage during playback, or a manually selected collage. Reduced motion initially loads only the first four images. The first collage works without JavaScript.

Deploy the complete `public/images/welcome/collage/` directory and the updated public credits to both Hostinger public directories with the welcome partial, CSS and JavaScript before rebuilding views. No migration or configuration change is required. This update is local; it has not been deployed.
