Using the satellite field check

In Farmers → Parcel map, select a farmer and expand the saved parcel's Land Plots box. Choose Satellite / NDVI, also labeled Satellite field check. The analysis opens in an in-page modal; a full-page fallback remains available.

Choose NDVI for general greenness, NDMI to investigate possible vegetation water stress, SAVI for sparse or newly planted crops, or EVI for dense vegetation. Choose a period of up to 31 days and a cloud limit, then select Find pictures. Changing the index clears old results without automatically contacting the provider. Each explicit search processes only the chosen index. The parcel feature uses Sentinel-2 observations through Copernicus Data Space and can show cloud-masked index colours, true-color imagery and usable-pixel summaries. Provider configuration and a shared local request allowance are required.

NDMI uses B08 and B11 on an approximately 20 m output grid because B11 has 20 m source resolution. NDVI, SAVI, EVI and natural-colour views use approximately 10 m output cells; cloud classification is 20 m. SAVI uses a fixed soil adjustment L = 0.5. Statistics and maps use harmonized reflectance, not raw digital numbers. SAVI and EVI measurements can exceed the −1 to +1 colour scale; exact values and expanded numeric charts are retained. Blank pixels have no usable reading, not a zero score.

Review dates and valid coverage before interpreting any index. These are vegetation signals, not a diagnosis or yield measurement. NDMI is not soil-moisture percentage or proof of drought: compare crop stage, rainfall, irrigation and field inspection. The climate dashboard's saved NDVI summary remains NDVI-only; other indices do not set risk levels. The Assistant cannot fetch a fresh satellite observation or inspect the selected parcel. Do not repeatedly refresh an error; contact the administrator when the shared request allowance is reached.
