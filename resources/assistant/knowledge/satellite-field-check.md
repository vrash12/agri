Using the satellite field check

In Farmers → Parcel map, select a farmer and expand the saved parcel's Land Plots box. Choose Satellite / NDVI, also labeled Satellite field check. The analysis opens in an in-page modal; a full-page fallback remains available.

Choose a period of up to 31 days and a cloud limit. The parcel feature uses Sentinel-2 observations through Copernicus Data Space and can show cloud-masked NDVI, true-color imagery and clear-pixel summaries for the saved parcel. Provider configuration and a local request allowance are required.

Review the observation dates and valid coverage before interpreting the reading. A cloudy or missing observation is not zero crop health. NDVI is a vegetation signal, not a diagnosis or yield measurement. The Assistant cannot fetch a fresh satellite observation or inspect the selected parcel. Do not repeatedly refresh an error; contact the administrator when the request allowance is reached.
