Supported Assistant questions

The signed-in office Assistant is one conversation without mode or year forms. State the reporting year and choose a farmer when prompted. New chat and leaving the page clear browser-memory chat. It never changes records.

Software help covers farmer profiles/imports, parcels, planted crop sections, agriculture/fisheries releases, rice sheets/import errors, assistance coverage, satellite/NDVI, multiple-animal services, role access, farmer sign-in/cards and harvest entry. It also explains dashboards/provider usage, weather/PAGASA, cooperatives, machinery, protected backups, audits, geofences and data quality. Availability follows the user's role. Identity/AI-technology questions explain the Assistant; guidance does not claim actions completed.

Local read-only tasks include current farmer/mapping totals, annual assistance/harvest reports, workspace summaries, farmer search and selected farmer parcels/crops/assistance/harvests. Existing permissions govern fresh local reads. Records, raw questions and chat never enter provider history; identity/greetings are local. Help sends only canonical application-owned questions and reviewed guidance to OpenAI. Unsupported tasks, geographic subsets, mixed requests and year comparisons clarify. There is no unrelated general chat, live weather, web search or record-writing tool. Never paste secrets or confidential records/documents.

After software help, ask for steps, a shorter explanation or an example. “In Filipino” or “In English” repeats the same approved topic in that language; records are not sent for translation. Recognized crop, season, month, gender, mapping, species or service filters require the relevant register instead of broader chat totals.

Follow-up buttons fill an editable message; review it and press Send. They never overwrite unfinished text. After selecting a farmer, assistance or harvest requests may ask “Selected farmer” or “Whole office” and retain the year. A year-only reply keeps scope pending. Farmer matches use separate choices; every record read checks permissions again.
