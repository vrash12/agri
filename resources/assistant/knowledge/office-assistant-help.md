Supported Assistant questions

The signed-in office Assistant is one conversation with no help/report selector or year form. Ask a question, state the reporting year in the message and clarify a farmer when prompted. New chat and leaving the page clear the browser-memory conversation. The Assistant never changes records.

Software help covers parcel drawing/import, planted crop sections, rice release import errors, satellite/NDVI limits, multiple-animal service recording, role access, farmer sign-in, registry cards and harvest entry. It explains reviewed application workflows rather than claiming an action completed.

Local read-only tasks include current farmer/mapping totals, assistance and harvest reports for one year, a workspace summary, farmer search and selected farmer parcel/crop/assistance/harvest summaries. Those figures are queried with existing permissions inside AgriLGU. Private records and the visible conversation are not sent to OpenAI. Unknown tasks, geographic subsets, mixed requests and year comparisons require clarification. Do not paste secrets or private documents into chat. The Assistant has no live weather, web search or record-writing tools.
