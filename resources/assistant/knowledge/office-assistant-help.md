Supported Assistant questions

The signed-in Assistant is one conversation without mode or year forms. State the reporting year and choose a farmer when prompted. It never changes records.

Software help covers registry/imports, parcels, crop drawing, assistance and rice sheets, satellite indices, climate readiness, animal services, account scope, farmer access/cards, harvest entry, dashboards, weather, cooperatives, machinery, backups, audits and geofences. Availability follows the role. General agriculture education covers soil, rotation, nutrients, seeds, planting preparation, irrigation, pest management, livestock and aquaculture. This is not field diagnosis, exact treatment, live prices or current weather.

Record questions run locally: farmer/mapping totals, retained workbook sections, Outside LGU and Delisted rows, seasonal crop counts, annual assistance/harvests/Animal Health, cooperatives, machinery, LGU profile setup, workspace summaries, farmer search and selected farmer details. Each read checks permissions. Answers identify source registers, account scope, reporting period and when the query ran; this is not proof that stored data is complete. Records, raw questions and chat never enter provider history. OpenAI receives canonical topics and reviewed guides only. Unrelated questions and unsupported tasks clarify locally. There is no web search or record-writing tool.

After reviewed help, ask for steps, a shorter explanation or an example. In Filipino or In English repeats that approved topic; private records are never sent for translation. Crop, season, month, gender, mapping, species or service filters require the relevant register instead of broader totals.

Follow-up buttons fill editable messages for explicit Send and preserve drafts. After selecting a farmer, assistance/harvest requests may ask Selected farmer or Whole office and retain the year. A year-only reply keeps scope pending. Farmer matches use separate choices; each subsequent read rechecks permissions. New chat or leaving clears browser-memory chat. Never paste secrets or confidential documents.
