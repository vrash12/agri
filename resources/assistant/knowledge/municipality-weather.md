Municipality weather and official bulletins

In Farmers, open Parcel map and its weather panel. Select an authorized municipality when your role permits location selection; municipal accounts remain in their assigned municipality. The panel shows municipality-level conditions, rainfall and rain probability, wind/gusts, a three-day outlook and rule-based farming guidance. These forecasts use configured town reference points, not measurements at each parcel.

Check the displayed update time and availability message. Forecasts are cached from Open-Meteo. A provider outage may show an explicitly identified last-known forecast; an unavailable municipality can have no configured reference point. Use the panel's refresh action when appropriate, rather than repeatedly refreshing errors.

The panel's PAGASA weather, tropical-cyclone, flood and agri-weather links lead to official bulletins. System guidance is not an official warning or a field inspection. Consult the responsible local agriculture and disaster authorities for urgent operational decisions. The Assistant explains the panel but does not fetch current weather or answer whether a specific farm is safe today. Do not present cached forecasts as a live, parcel-specific reading.
