Crops need multiple nutrients. Nitrogen supports vegetative growth; phosphorus supports energy transfer and root development; potassium supports several plant functions including water regulation. NPK denotes these three nutrients. Plants also need other nutrients.

Fertilizer requirements depend on the crop, growing conditions and the nutrients already supplied by the soil and other inputs. The same fertilizer rate is not appropriate for every rice or corn field. Site-specific nutrient management adjusts recommendations to the field and cropping conditions.

Use appropriate soil or crop assessment and local extension guidance to decide the product, amount and timing. Excess nutrients can waste money and harm the environment. Do not infer a deficiency from yellow leaves alone. This guide gives no diagnosis, bag count, fertilizer rate or promised yield.
