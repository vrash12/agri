Parcel and municipality reference limits

Saved farm-parcel boundaries and official municipality geofences are separate records. Municipality geofences are planning references, not evidence of legal ownership or a national land title. Drawing a parcel or importing KML/KMZ does not establish ownership or registration eligibility.

Selecting a region, province or municipality filter cannot extend the signed-in account's access. The server applies role and location policies before parcel reads or writes. Missing or invalid geometry requires review in the parcel form; a displayed map is not proof that it was saved.

Parcel crop sections classify planted areas for a selected year and season. They cannot leave the parcel or overlap other sections. Map area measurements remain approximate. Use verified local records and field checks when the boundary or area is uncertain. The Assistant has no drawing tools and cannot inspect coordinates or uploaded boundary files.
