Parcel and municipality reference limits

Saved farm-parcel boundaries and official municipality geofences are separate records. Municipality geofences are planning references, not evidence of legal ownership or a national land title. Drawing a parcel or importing KML/KMZ does not establish ownership or registration eligibility.

Selecting a region, province or municipality filter cannot extend the signed-in account's access. The server applies role and location policies before parcel reads or writes. Missing or invalid geometry requires review in the parcel form; a displayed map is not proof that it was saved.

Parcel crop marks identify the land planted with a selected crop for a selected year and season. Staff can draw a visual line with two or more points; these marks do not calculate hectares. Unplanted parts of a parcel can remain blank. A crop mark cannot leave the saved parcel. Existing enclosed crop areas remain supported, and their approximate mapped hectares are calculated on save. If the saved parcel outline is wrong, update it before recording the crop mark. Use verified local records and field checks when the boundary or area is uncertain. The Assistant has no drawing tools and cannot inspect coordinates or uploaded boundary files.
