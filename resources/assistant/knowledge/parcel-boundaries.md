Drawing or importing a farm parcel

Open Farmers, choose Parcel map and search for or select the farmer. Draw boundary records the whole farm parcel. Draw the parcel corners, finish the shape, review the farmer and boundary details, then use the original form's save action. KML/KMZ import accepts reviewed parcel geometry as another way to add a farm boundary.

Land Plots use compact expandable boxes. Open a plot box to reveal Focus, Satellite / NDVI, Seasonal crops and download/print actions available to the current role. Seasonal crops draws planted sections within a saved parcel; it does not create a new whole parcel.

Municipal staff and heads work within their assigned municipality. Provincial agriculture staff choose an authorized municipality when creating records. Oversight accounts review operational records without routine parcel writes. Correct geometry and ownership errors in the original form. The Assistant cannot draw, upload, import or save a parcel and cannot confirm that a change completed.
