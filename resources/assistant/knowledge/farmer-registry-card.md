Farmer registry card printing and signatures

Open the farmer record and choose its existing ID-card action. Authorized users can preview the front and back, print the card and export PNG artwork. Screen, print and PNG use the same card artwork. The card is an office registry card, not a national government ID.

The front carries farmer identity. The back carries a QR and a cardholder signature line above the printed name. An office signature line appears only for configured municipalities. Signatures are written in ink after printing; no signature image is uploaded or stored. Do not claim an unsigned card is already signed.

Farm locations use imported parcel addresses separated by slashes. Residence addresses are not substituted for missing parcel addresses. Missing or oversized addresses require office review; long addresses can refer to the full printed sheet rather than being silently dropped. Office marks and signatory lines follow the municipality's current configuration. Protected fields and the QR destination keep their existing access rules. The Assistant cannot issue, sign or print a card itself.
