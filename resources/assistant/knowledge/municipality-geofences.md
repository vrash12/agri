Municipality geofence references

Open Municipality geofences to choose an authorized municipality and inspect its boundary and available barangay reference layers. Municipality geofences and farmer parcels are different records. The mapped administrative outlines are planning references requiring responsible LGU/NAMRIA validation; they are not legal titles, ownership evidence or eligibility decisions.

System Owners may manage geofences across their permitted configured scopes. Super Admins may manage them only in their assigned province. Regional Heads and operational staff review references without geofence editing. Veterinary accounts cannot use this module. Dedicated visitors and GIS evaluators have separate read-only boundary restrictions and cannot use the office Assistant.

Authorized geofence managers draw or import reviewed KML, KMZ, GeoJSON, JSON or XML boundary geometry through the original form. Review the selected municipality, draft/active status, geometry validation and replacement confirmation. Only one boundary can remain active for a municipality; a confirmed replacement archives the preceding active version. Stale-edit messages require reviewing the latest record.

Saved boundary color and fill opacity apply to Municipality geofences and the Farmers parcel map; reload an already-open parcel map after changing style. A permitted municipality snapshot is a map export, not a legal boundary certificate. The Assistant can explain these controls but cannot draw, import, activate, archive or validate a boundary itself.
