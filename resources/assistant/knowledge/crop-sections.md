Drawing planted crop sections

In Farmers, open Parcel map, find or select the farmer and expand the saved parcel's Land Plots box. Choose Seasonal crops / Crop areas / season. This opens an in-page dialog while preserving the parcel map; the standalone seasonal-crop page is also available.

Choose the crop year and dry or wet season. Draw each planted section inside the existing saved parcel, using the numbered corner dots and connecting lines. Select the crop and, when needed, a section name or variety. Finish the boundary, review its shape and save the season records.

The editor supports separate corn and rice sections within one parcel. Saved crop icons identify the recorded classification on the selected farmer's map and season; they do not show live growth. Authorized writing roles may save. System Owner, Regional Head and Super Admin oversight is read-only for crop records. The Assistant cannot draw, save or change a section. Individual crop-section linkage to harvest records is not implemented.
