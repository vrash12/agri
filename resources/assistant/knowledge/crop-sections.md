Drawing planted crop sections

In Farmers, open Parcel map, find or select the farmer and expand the saved parcel's Land Plots box. Choose Seasonal crops / Crop areas / season. This opens an in-page dialog while preserving the parcel map; the standalone seasonal-crop page is also available.

Choose the crop year and dry or wet season. Select a crop and Start crop line, then click two or more points along the planted section inside the existing saved parcel. Optional names or varieties identify the mark. Finish the crop line and save the season. Lines are visual crop marks without hectare calculations; older enclosed crop areas remain editable.

The editor supports separate corn and rice marks within one parcel. Saved crop lines, shaded sections and icons appear automatically across loaded parcels for the selected year and season, without clicking a farmer. White outlines mark parcels. A saved whole-parcel crop without drawn sections adds its crop-color tint and icon; separate crop drawings color only their saved shapes. No saved record means a white parcel without a crop icon. A whole-parcel tint identifies the seasonal crop, not measured planted hectares. Zoom in for section icons; zoomed out, multiple crops share a parcel badge. Click a crop section to inspect its farmer while keeping the whole map visible. Use the progress message and Retry if some drawings fail to load. Icons do not show live growth. Authorized writing roles may save. System Owner, Regional Head and Super Admin oversight is read-only for crop records. The Assistant cannot draw, save or change a section. A linked harvest prevents that section from being removed or reclassified until the harvest link is cleared.
