Crop boundary and saving rules

A season record accepts up to 8 crop marks with 2–50 points per visual line. Every line segment must stay within the saved parcel. Simple lines with three or more corners may receive display-only shading if their closing edge also stays inside the parcel. Shaded sections cannot overlap, but may share edges. Two-point and crossing marks remain visual lines without hectares. Existing enclosed areas retain 3–50 corners and non-overlap validation. Use Mixed crops for intercropping in one enclosed area rather than overlapping corn and rice polygons.

Unmarked land remains unclassified. Visual crop lines do not calculate hectares; existing polygon measurements are approximate and do not replace verified measurements. Every saved parcel base on the Farmers map uses a white outline and pale white fill, including parcels with only a crop classification. Crop icons and separate saved crop sections retain their crop colors above that base. Stored parcel colors, full boundaries, hectares and export data remain unchanged; crop filters, hover feedback and edit visibility still apply. Overview icons describe saved seasonal classifications, not a measurement of planted land or proof of live growth.

If another staff member changes the season record, the stale-edit message requires reloading and reviewing the latest version before saving. Do not bypass it or assume the earlier drawing was stored. Staff must use an authorized municipality and parcel; a map filter does not grant access. The Assistant explains these rules but cannot validate or persist a drawing.
