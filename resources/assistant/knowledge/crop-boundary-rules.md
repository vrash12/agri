Crop boundary and saving rules

A season record accepts up to 8 crop marks with 2–50 points per visual line. Every line segment must stay within the saved parcel. Line crossings are permitted because they do not define area ownership or hectares. Existing enclosed areas retain 3–50 corners and non-overlap validation. Use Mixed crops for intercropping in one enclosed area rather than overlapping corn and rice polygons.

Unmarked land remains unclassified. Visual crop lines do not calculate hectares; existing polygon measurements are approximate and do not replace verified measurements. The saved parcel boundary and its original display color remain unchanged by crop styling. Overview icons describe saved seasonal classifications, not a measurement of planted land or proof of live growth.

If another staff member changes the season record, the stale-edit message requires reloading and reviewing the latest version before saving. Do not bypass it or assume the earlier drawing was stored. Staff must use an authorized municipality and parcel; a map filter does not grant access. The Assistant explains these rules but cannot validate or persist a drawing.
