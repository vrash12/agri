Crop boundary and saving rules

A season record accepts up to 8 planted sections with 3–50 corners per section. Every section must lie within the saved parcel. Sections may touch, but their interiors cannot overlap. Use Mixed crops for intercropping in the same area rather than overlapping corn and rice drawings.

Unmarked land remains unclassified. Mapped hectares are approximate and do not replace verified measurements. The saved parcel boundary and its original display color remain unchanged by crop styling.

If another staff member changes the season record, the stale-edit message requires reloading and reviewing the latest version before saving. Do not bypass it or assume the earlier drawing was stored. Staff must use an authorized municipality and parcel; a map filter does not grant access. The Assistant explains these rules but cannot validate or persist a drawing.
