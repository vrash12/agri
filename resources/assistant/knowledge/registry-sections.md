Outside LGU and Delisted workbook references

Open Farmers, then choose Outside LGU or Delisted in the farmer workspace navigation. The section uses the signed-in account's existing permitted area. Location, search and page-size controls affect the displayed retained source rows. Expand Original fields to inspect the approved workbook's recorded values. These are workbook rows, not a count of unique farmers; repeated parcels or commodities can occupy separate rows.

Outside LGU describes an imported reference status. It does not move ownership to another municipality or grant access to that municipality. An active farmer link is shown only when the linked profile belongs to the same registry municipality. Delisted references do not provide active-profile or farmer-ID actions, even if an old farmer link remains. A delisted source row does not itself prove that a separate current profile was removed.

Missing information is not invented. These pages display already retained source records; opening them does not reimport a workbook, update a farmer or create an account. The normal workbook importer does not automatically retain every sheet. Follow the approved import workflow and ask the office administrator to investigate missing source rows.

Ask the Assistant to summarize registry sections, outside LGU rows or delisted rows for a fresh, local scoped count. The answer identifies its source and limits. Current retained rows are not a historical annual snapshot. Use the section itself for original fields and detailed searches. Veterinary, visitor, evaluator and farmer-portal accounts do not gain access through chat.
