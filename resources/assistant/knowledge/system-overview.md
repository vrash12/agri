eAgriLGU system overview

eAgriLGU is an agriculture information and GIS management application for local government agriculture offices. It combines municipality-owned farmer registry records and cards, farm-parcel maps, dry/wet seasonal crop sections, agriculture/fisheries assistance, harvest records, Animal Health, cooperatives, machinery, protected documents, dashboards, reports and authorized administration.

Office users sign in to an assigned role and active region, province or municipality. Municipal roles work within their municipality; provincial and regional roles remain within their assigned scope; System Owner oversight covers configured provinces. Oversight is read-only for routine operational records. Veterinary accounts are Animal Health-only. Server policies enforce these limits even when a URL or filter is changed.

Farmers use the separate Farmer sign-in option and their own portal to review their profile, owned parcel map, assistance and harvest history. They cannot browse another farmer's records or use office administration. Application branding does not change stored registry identifiers.

The office Assistant explains reviewed software workflows and reads its supported authorized local record summaries. It does not perform office transactions, validate land ownership or replace agriculture-office verification. A direct harvest can carry an explicit planted-section attribution, but that link does not prove yield, hectares or live growth. Unknown questions, missing records and unsupported reporting combinations must be acknowledged rather than filled in with invented information. No automatic national-system interoperability or deployment to every LGU is claimed.
