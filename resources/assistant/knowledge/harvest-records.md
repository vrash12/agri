Entering harvest records

Open Harvest Records and use the entry form for the recorded commodity, year, season/date, harvested area and quantity with its unit. Choose an authorized municipality and beneficiary when the form requires them. Review the actual measurements before saving; the Assistant cannot create or modify the record.

A harvest recorded through a seed release can be linked to that release. Release-projected records are managed through their release. Direct harvest entries remain separate. A seed release is assistance delivered, while a planted crop section describes a recorded planted boundary. Neither automatically proves harvested production.

Direct harvest entries may optionally link to one saved planted section from the same farmer and parcel. The section key is an attribution only: it does not infer hectares, quantity, yield or a seed release. The year, season and compatible crop must match; release-projected rice harvests remain linked only to their release. Do not treat NDVI as a harvest measurement. Preserve quantity units instead of combining kilograms, sacks and pieces. Local Assistant reports can summarize permitted recorded harvests for one year, including missing quantities, but cannot infer absent measurements. Resolve validation and stale-edit errors in the original form.
