Farmer portal sign-in and assisted recovery

Authorized staff issue or recover portal access from the farmer's profile/history → Farmer portal access after verifying identity through the office workflow. Use that original form; the Assistant cannot create access or reset a password. Passwords cannot be retrieved from the system.

Farmers use the separate Farmer sign-in option on the shared sign-in page. They can view only their own My Profile, parcels, assistance and harvest history through the separate farmer session. Office and farmer sessions have different permissions. There is no public farmer registration or birthday-based recovery.

Do not paste passwords, contact details, identity documents or private farmer fields into chat. If sign-in fails, authorized office staff should review the farmer's account status and municipality assignment in the original workflow. The Assistant cannot unlock a session, see credentials or confirm a completed account update. Existing registry identifiers remain unchanged by application branding.
