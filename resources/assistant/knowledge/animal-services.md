Recording multiple animals and services

Open Animal Health and use the service form. Staff can record multiple animal or group entries under one owner, for example two dogs and one cow. Keep dogs and cattle as separate entries with their own species and animal counts rather than treating them as one animal.

Record the actual vaccination, deworming, vitamins or treatment service for each entry. Review the owner, authorized municipality and each animal/group before saving through the original form. Required or invalid fields must be corrected there. A veterinary account may record Animal Health services only within its assigned province and must choose an authorized municipality when creating records.

The Assistant can explain the workflow and show permitted local Animal Health summaries for one year. It cannot enter or change services, verify a save, diagnose an animal or choose medication doses. Clinical decisions belong to the responsible veterinarian.
