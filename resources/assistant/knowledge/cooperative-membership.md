Cooperative profiles and member lists

Open Farmers Cooperative to search cooperative profiles and review membership totals. Membership filters distinguish groups with assigned members from groups needing membership. Authorized operational staff create or edit a cooperative in the owning municipality through the original form. Oversight accounts can review permitted records but cannot perform routine cooperative edits.

Open Manage members for an existing cooperative, review the selected farmers and save the membership list. Only farmers from that cooperative's municipality can be assigned. Saving synchronizes the selected membership, so check the whole selection before submitting. A stale-edit message requires reloading and reviewing the latest list. Membership changes are audited.

A cooperative with assigned members cannot be moved to another municipality until those members are removed through the authorized workflow. Deleting a cooperative removes its profile and membership links while retaining farmer records. An authorized member-list export produces an Excel workbook. Check the active scope and filters before exporting, and handle the resulting member information through the office's approved process. The Assistant explains the workflow; it cannot assign members, change profiles, export a file or inspect cooperative records through an AI provider.
