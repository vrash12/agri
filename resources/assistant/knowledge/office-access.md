Office role and location access

Municipal Staff work in their assigned municipality. Municipal Heads also manage municipal staff in that municipality. Provincial Agriculture Staff work across authorized municipalities in their province and choose the owning municipality when creating records.

Super Administrators oversee one province and manage permitted accounts, security and geofences; routine operational records are read-only. Regional Heads oversee their assigned region, manage authorized lower accounts and cannot edit operational records or geofences. System Owners oversee configured provinces, accounts and geofences, with read-only routine operations. Veterinary accounts use Animal Health only.

The server enforces account scope and record policies. Filters and Assistant context do not grant access. Managers cannot change their own privileges or protected peer/owner accounts. Passwords cannot be retrieved. Recovery is assisted by authorized office staff. The Assistant is read-only and cannot unlock accounts or change permissions. Restricted visitors, GIS evaluators and farmer sessions cannot use the office Assistant.
