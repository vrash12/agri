Planting a real crop is different from drawing its area on the AgriLGU map. Before planting rice, corn or vegetables, consider these general planning steps:

1. Choose a crop and variety suited to the season, soil, available water and intended use.
2. Assess field conditions, drainage and soil constraints with local agricultural guidance.
3. Obtain suitable quality seed or planting material.
4. Plan establishment, water, nutrients, weed management and labour for that crop.
5. Observe emergence and keep notes for field review.

Rice, corn and vegetables have different establishment and water needs. This general guide does not give a universal seed rate, spacing, planting date, fertilizer dose or guaranteed yield. Ask the municipal agriculture office for recommendations suited to the crop and local conditions.
