Assistant help for Animal Health

The veterinary Assistant is one conversation for Animal Health software help and permitted local Animal Health reports. State a reporting year in the message; there is no help/report selector or year form. It can explain multiple animal/group entries under one owner and recorded vaccination, deworming, vitamins and treatment services.

The veterinary account remains within its assigned province and Animal Health permissions. Reports use authorized local records and do not enter OpenAI prompts. The visible conversation stays in browser memory; New chat and leaving the page clear it. Follow-up references are short-lived and do not grant access.

The Assistant cannot create services, change records, unlock an account, retrieve passwords or verify a save. Unknown, mixed and year-comparison requests require clarification. Do not paste owner contacts, private files or credentials into chat. Clinical diagnosis, medication doses and treatment choices require the responsible veterinarian. For a permission problem, ask the authorized administrator to review the assignment.
