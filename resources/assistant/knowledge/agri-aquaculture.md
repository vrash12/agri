Aquaculture farms aquatic organisms such as fish. Successful fish-farm management brings together suitable water, healthy stock, feeding, observation and records. Requirements differ by species, life stage and production system.

1. Assess the water supply and monitor its quality with suitable methods.
2. Match stocking and feed management to the species and system with fisheries guidance.
3. Observe fish behaviour, feeding response and mortality, and investigate changes promptly.
4. Keep stocking, feed, water and harvest records to review management.

Do not guess stocking density, feed amounts, chemical treatments or water-quality limits. Seek a fisheries technician for a specific pond or unusual fish behaviour. This chat has no current pond measurements and cannot diagnose fish disease or confirm a harvest.
