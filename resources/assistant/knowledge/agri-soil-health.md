Healthy soil supports roots, holds and drains water, cycles nutrients and contains living organisms. Texture, structure, organic matter and chemical conditions all matter. Soil health cannot be judged from colour alone.

1. Learn the soil conditions through field observation and appropriate soil testing with the agriculture office.
2. Maintain organic matter with suitable organic materials, including properly prepared compost where appropriate.
3. Use suitable crop residues or cover crops to protect the surface and reduce erosion and evaporation.
4. Avoid unnecessary compaction and manage drainage.

Compost is decomposed organic material; it does not automatically supply every nutrient a crop needs. Soil amendments and quantities depend on the soil and crop. No soil test, soil pH or deficiency has been measured by this chat.
