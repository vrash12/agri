Agriculture includes growing crops and raising farm animals for food, feed and other useful products. Farming supports food supply, livelihoods and raw materials. Crop production includes cereals such as rice and corn, vegetables, fruit and other crops. Livestock and poultry production raises animals; aquaculture farms aquatic organisms such as fish. Mixed farming combines activities such as crops and livestock. Subsistence farming mainly meets household needs; commercial farming mainly produces for sale.

A farming system must fit the soil, water, climate, available labour and intended use of the produce. Sustainable farming aims to maintain production while caring for soil, water and living resources. These are general concepts, not a recommendation for a particular farm or an official classification of its records.
