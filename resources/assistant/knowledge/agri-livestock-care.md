General livestock and poultry care includes suitable feed and clean water, appropriate shelter, hygiene and daily observation. Feed and water should be protected from contamination. Needs differ by species, age and production stage.

Biosecurity reduces opportunities for disease to enter and spread. Keep equipment and housing clean, manage waste, and use suitable separation and animal-movement practices with veterinary guidance. Observe changes in appetite, behaviour and condition; seek a veterinarian when an animal is ill or several animals are affected.

This is general education, not an animal examination or diagnosis. Do not provide medicine, antibiotic, vaccine or dewormer doses. These depend on veterinary assessment and approved instructions. Understanding husbandry does not grant access to another office’s animal records.
