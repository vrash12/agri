Assistance and harvest quantities

Agriculture & Fisheries records actual releases of seed, fertilizer, fingerlings, feed and fishing gear. Harvest Records records measured harvested production. An assistance release is not automatically a harvest and is not a planted crop section.

Keep each quantity's recorded unit. Kilograms, pieces, sacks and other units are not interchangeable, so a report must not combine them into one quantity. Assistance and harvest summaries are for the requested record year; farmer and mapping totals are current registry totals, not historical snapshots.

A seed-release harvest can be linked to that release. Release-projected harvest rows are managed through the release, while directly entered harvest rows remain separate. A direct harvest may also reference one saved planted section when its farmer, parcel, year, season and compatible crop match; this records attribution only and never infers production. The Assistant can return authorized local report totals but cannot infer missing production, convert unsupported units or create a release or harvest record.
