Animal Health advice and record limits

Animal Health supports vaccination, deworming, vitamins and treatment records. Species, animal counts and service entries represent office records; service totals are not necessarily a count of distinct animals. Record the actual service rather than inventing a diagnosis or dosage.

The Assistant gives software help and authorized read-only local Animal Health reports. Animal diagnosis, medication dose and treatment decisions require the responsible veterinarian. It has no clinical assessment tools and cannot verify that a service was delivered or a record was saved.

Veterinary accounts stay within Animal Health and their assigned province. Choosing a municipality never grants access outside that assignment. For a missing field, ownership error or outdated-record message, return to the original form and review the latest record. Do not paste owner contact details, private documents or credentials into the conversation.
