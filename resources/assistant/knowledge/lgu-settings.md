LGU office profiles, ID issuers and public websites

Open Settings, then LGU profiles & IDs. The list includes active offices in the account's permitted area, including offices without a profile. System Owners, scoped provincial Super Administrators and municipal heads may maintain profiles. Other permitted office users have scoped read access; veterinary, evaluator, visitor and farmer-portal identities have no access.

An office profile can supply its name, official website, office contact details, address, description, location, logo and optional printed signatory name and title. Logos are inspected PNG, JPEG or WebP uploads; do not use SVG or remote image URLs. A blank saved signatory pair omits the office signature line. Maintain the current office head in Settings. Cards are signed in ink; saved signature images are not used. Existing printed cards do not update themselves.

Manage LGU website configures the office's slug, publication, welcome content, services, announcements, hours and cover. Publication requires an active municipality and province with a saved profile. Only approved published content becomes public. Subdomain routing also needs provider DNS, hosting and HTTPS configuration; saving a slug alone cannot establish those services. LGU-branded sign-in leads to the existing secure central dashboard; a hostname never grants record permissions.

The Assistant can summarize profile and publication coverage for permitted offices. It does not publish pages, change office details, create accounts or configure hosting. Use the source settings screen to review and save authorized changes. Private uploads, drafts and credentials are not AI knowledge sources.
