Satellite coverage and interpretation limits

NDVI indicates a vegetation signal for valid satellite pixels. It does not diagnose crop disease, measure harvested yield, prove ownership or decide assistance eligibility. Missing or cloud-masked pixels are unavailable data, not evidence of crop damage. Check the reading date, clear coverage, field conditions and responsible staff's inspection together.

NDMI indicates vegetation water content; water, bare soil and crop stage affect it, so no universal drought threshold is applied. SAVI reduces soil-background influence with L = 0.5 and does not measure nutrients. EVI helps dense-canopy comparisons where NDVI can saturate. Neither is a crop-health grade. Near-zero denominators, missing bands and non-finite calculations are masked; values are not silently clamped to the colour scale. NDMI uses approximately 20 m cells, while the other indices use approximately 10 m; the cloud mask is 20 m in every case. Sparse, narrow parcels may have very few usable cells.

The System Owner and Super Admin usage panel counts shared satellite request reservations across all indices and outgoing Google Static Maps attempts. It does not report complete Google billing, interactive Maps JavaScript loads or Copernicus processing units. Local allowances are safeguards rather than a provider invoice; cached readings do not consume a new request allowance.

The municipality weather drawer contains cached Open-Meteo forecasts and rule-based guidance. It is not an official warning. Use its PAGASA links for official bulletins and local disaster authorities for urgent decisions. The Assistant has no live forecast, satellite fetch or web search.
