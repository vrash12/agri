Veterinary account access

A Provincial Veterinary account has Animal Health-only access within its assigned active province. It chooses an authorized municipality when creating animal service records. The server enforces account scope and policies; a filter or an Assistant follow-up cannot grant access outside that assignment.

Animal Health supports vaccination, deworming, vitamins and treatment, with separate animal/group entries under an owner and species/counts per entry. The Assistant can explain that recording workflow and provide permitted Animal Health reports for one year. It cannot change records, manage accounts, unlock access or retrieve passwords.

The veterinary Assistant does not expose other operational modules. For an account or ownership problem, ask the authorized administrator to review the assignment. For an outdated-record message, review the latest version in the original Animal Health form. Clinical diagnosis, medication doses and treatment decisions belong to the responsible veterinarian.
